Part 1
Shear (剪切)
Summary.
  • Shear deformation (剪切变形): two equal, opposite forces act close together across a member, like scissors. The material either side of the plane between them slides, so a small rectangle abcd becomes a parallelogram.
  • Shear force (剪力): cut at the plane that slides. For a single shear plane, Fs = F. The force lies in the cut face.
  • Nominal shear stress (名义剪应力): τ = Fs / A, with A the area of the sliding plane. "Nominal" because it assumes τ is spread evenly. σ is perpendicular to the face, τ is parallel to it.
  • Strength condition (剪切强度条件): τ = Fs/A ≤ [τ]. Same three jobs as the axial version: check, size a section, find the allowable load.
  • Punching example: F = 100 kN, t = d = 10 mm, τb = 300 MPa. Sheared area is the side surface A = πdt = 314 mm² (not πd²/4). Resistance τbA = 94.2 kN < 100 kN, so the hole is cut. In punching you want τ to reach τb.
Part 2
Beam internal forces (梁的弯曲内力) · 3.1–3.3
Summary.
  • Beam (梁): a member that mainly bends. Plane bending (平面弯曲): loads in the plane of symmetry, so the axis curves within that plane. Loads: concentrated force P, concentrated couple m, distributed load q (resultant = area of the load diagram, at its centroid).
  • Internal forces at a cut: Fs (剪力) across the cut and M (弯矩) turning the face. Find reactions → cut → keep one side → ΣY = 0, ΣM = 0 about the cut.
  • Sign rules: Fs + when it turns the kept piece clockwise (顺正逆负). M + when the bottom is in tension, beam sags (下拉为正). Cantilevers are negative.
  • Shortcut (简便求法): Fs = sum of vertical forces on one side (clockwise-turning = +). M = sum of moments of one side's forces about the cut (bottom-stretching = +).
  • Drawing: Fs diagram positive above the baseline. M diagram drawn on the tension side, so positive below the baseline.

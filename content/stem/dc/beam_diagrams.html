Beam Diagrams Drill

Shear force and bending moment in pictures: the sign rules, how each kind of load shapes the diagrams, three standard beams, then eight questions where you pick the right drawing.

1 · Sign rules

What "positive" looks like

+Fs: turns the piece clockwise

Up on the left face, down on the right face. 顺正逆负.

+M: bottom in tension (sags)

The beam smiles. A cantilever frowns, so its M is negative. 下拉为正.

Drawing rule: the Fs diagram puts + above the baseline. The M diagram is drawn on the tension side, so +M goes below the baseline.

2 · Load → shape

What each load does to the diagrams

LoadFs diagramM diagram
No load on the segment
Point load P
Couple m
Uniform load q

Point load: Fs jumps by P, M gets a sharp corner (尖角). Couple: Fs is unchanged, M jumps by m. Uniform load: Fs is a sloped line, M is a parabola.

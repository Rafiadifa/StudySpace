Sign rules (p.156)

ForcePositive when…
FN axialtension (pulls away from the cut)

They follow the deformation, so the left and right pieces always give the same sign.

Shortcut, no free-body diagram (p.160)

  • Fs = Σ vertical forces on one side. Left side: up = +. Right side: down = +.
  • M = Σ moments of one side about the cut. Anything that makes the bottom stretch = +.
  • Couples: on the left piece, clockwise = +. On the right piece, counterclockwise = +.

Procedure (p.180 / p.191)

  1. Reactions (check with ΣY).
  2. Split at loads, load ends, couples and supports. Find Fs and M at each control section.
  3. Shape of each segment from the differential relations.
  4. Extremes where Fs = 0.
Draw Fs with + above the baseline. Draw M on the tension side, so + goes below.

Traps from the error-spotting slides (p.188–190)

  • A middle hinge (中间铰) carries no moment: M = 0 there.
  • No concentrated force → no jump in Fs.
  • A support reaction is a concentrated force, so Fs jumps there.
  • Symmetric beam and loads → Fs antisymmetric, M symmetric (p.186).

Week 10 · count the constraints, then inspect the geometry

What this assignment is asking

Classify each arrangement as geometrically stable (几何不变), finitely movable (几何可变), or instantaneously unstable (瞬变). Then determine redundant constraints. The English line in the source incorrectly translates the third category as “statically determinate.” Determinacy is a separate issue: a stable structure with no redundancy is statically determinate.

1 · Decide what the pieces are

A pin-jointed bar truss consists of straight bars connected by hinges. Each free joint has two planar translations. A frame can contain rigid corners and curved rigid members; treat each continuous rigid assembly as a disc with three planar motions. Do not turn a rigid corner into a hinge merely because a line bends there. A crossing is connected only if the drawing specifies a joint.

Joint-and-bar model

For a planar ideal pin-jointed truss, j free joints supply 2j degrees of freedom; each bar and each independent scalar support restraint removes one.

W = 2j − b − r
Rigid-disc model

For n moving rigid discs, h simple pin connections between discs and c additional scalar constraints (including support constraints):

W = 3n − 2h − c

A common pin joining k discs supplies 2(k−1) constraints, not just two. If the ground is a disc, do not count the same supports again in c.

2 · Count support constraints correctly

A pin supplies two translations; a roller supplies one normal to its contact surface; a fixed end supplies three planar restraints. A two-force link supplies one along its own axis. A cable supplies one only while taut. A pin transfers forces but no couple; a fixed corner maintains the relative angle.

3 · Interpret the number, without overclaiming

W = m − s

Here m is the number of infinitesimal motion modes and s the number of redundant scalar constraints. Thus W > 0 proves that a motion exists. W = 0 allows either m=s=0 or a motion and a redundancy together. W < 0 gives at least −W redundancies, but still does not prove stability. Only after establishing m=0 may you conclude s=−W.

4 · Check geometry and assembly

Two rigid discs joined by three bars are stably connected if their three axes are neither concurrent nor all parallel. Two discs can also be connected by a pin and a bar whose axis does not pass through the pin. Three discs joined by three distinct non-collinear hinges form a stable assembly. Collinearity, concurrency, or parallelism can make these connections degenerate.

A new pin joint attached to a stable structure by two non-collinear bars is a stable two-bar addition. Remove such additions to simplify a large truss. Collinear bars do not restrain the transverse velocity of their shared joint to first order.

Instantaneous versus finite: a straight two-bar chain with fixed endpoints has a first-order sideways velocity at its middle joint, although finite movement is blocked by the fixed lengths. A non-degenerate unbraced four-bar loop has an actual finite mechanism. Both can have an infinitesimal mode; their finite behavior differs.

Worked contrast · same count, different geometry

Three pin joints connected by three bars and supported by a pin plus a roller give W=6−3−3=0. If the three joints form a triangle and the roller restrains overall rotation, m=s=0. If all three joints are collinear, the middle joint can move transversely to first order: m=1 and s=1. The count stays zero. The missing information is geometry.

Your solution format

  1. State the model and assumptions at joints and supports.
  2. Show the count without double-counting.
  3. Explain how stable pieces connect, or identify a specific permitted motion.
  4. State the category and redundancy, consistent with W=m−s.
